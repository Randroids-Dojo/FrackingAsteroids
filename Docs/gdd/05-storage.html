Storage

Status: partial

Storage is one of the three upgradeable ship systems (alongside Blaster and Collector). It governs how much cargo the hold can carry and a bonus applied to scrap. Storage advances through five tiers. Each tier raises the cargo capacity and grants an increasing scrap bonus.

Tier Capacity Scrap Bonus
1 50 0%
2 125 10%
3 250 20%
4 500 35%
5 1000 50%

The storage tier is part of the persisted upgrade set: Upgrades { blaster, collector, storage }, each an integer from 1 to 5. The cargo hold tracks fragments against capacity in the Cargo schema. New games start at storage tier 1 with a cargo capacity of 50.

Storage upgrades are purchased at the gas station Trade menu by spending scrap. The win condition requires reaching the maximum upgrade tier across all three systems, storage included.

Build log

  • Storage system shipped pre-migration as part of the Economy (M4) and Upgrades (M5) milestones. The persisted storage tier (1 to 5) and capacity field are defined in src/lib/schemas.ts with a default new-game cargo capacity of 50. Storage upgrade cost ladder (STORAGE_COSTS: tier 2 = 30, tier 3 = 100, tier 4 = 300, tier 5 = 800) lives in src/game/types.ts. Purchase flow runs through buyUpgrade in src/hooks/useGameState.ts and the Trade menu in src/components/TradeMenu.tsx; the cargo bar reads capacity in src/components/HUD.tsx. Schema defaults verified by tests/unit/schemas.test.ts and cost ladder by tests/unit/types.test.ts. Note: the per-tier capacity scaling (125, 250, 500, 1000) and per-tier scrap bonus (10%, 20%, 35%, 50%) from the spec are not yet applied in code: buyUpgrade only increments the tier number, and capacity stays at 50 with no scrap bonus logic.
