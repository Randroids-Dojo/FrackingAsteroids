Asteroids

Status: done

Asteroids are the core minable objects of the field. They come in five types, each with a distinct color palette, size, and HP value. They are voxel models with seeded shapes for visual variety, drift across the field at varying speeds and angles, and are spawned in a ring around the gas station after the tutorial.

  • Crystalline asteroids are immune to the standard blaster and require a Lazer to mine.
  • Asteroids drift across the field at varying speeds and angles.
  • Spawned in a ring around the gas station after the tutorial.
  • Voxel models with seeded shapes for visual variety.

Moon-size asteroids

Size 0 asteroids (moon) are prologue-only, with voxel size 3.0, collision radius 16, and HP ranging from 25 (precious) to 80 (crystalline). They use 5 voxel layers for imposing depth.
