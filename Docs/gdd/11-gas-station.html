Gas Station

Status: done

The gas station is a fixed structure in the world that serves two gameplay functions: drive-through repair and trade. It is rendered as a voxel "Moon's Drive-In" style space station with neon signage.

Location

  • Located north of the starting area.

Drive-through repair

  • Drive-through repair: restores HP to full when passing close.

The player ship has 100 HP. HP is lost when hit by enemy projectiles. HP is restored to full when passing through the gas station (drive-through repair). HP is persisted across saves.

Trade menu

  • Trade menu: sell materials for scrap, buy upgrades.

The trade menu lets the player sell collected materials (silver and gold) for scrap currency, and spend scrap to buy upgrades for the blaster, collector, and storage systems, as well as the Lazer mining tool.

Upgrade tiers reachable at the station

The trade menu progresses each system across the tier tables defined in the ship systems spec.
