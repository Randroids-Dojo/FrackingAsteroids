Audio

Status: done

All audio is procedurally synthesized using the Web Audio API. There are no external audio files. The system is split into background music, sound effects, and shared audio context handling.

Background Music

Procedural synthesis (no external files)

Every sound in the game, both music and SFX, is generated live through the Web Audio API. No sample files, streams, or external audio assets are loaded.

Four-layer procedural soundtrack

The background music is a four-layer procedural soundtrack generated live. Each layer fades in as overall intensity rises.

LayerSoundBehavior
Layer 0Deep bass droneAlways playing
Layer 1Pad chordsFades in at low intensity
Layer 2Arpeggiated melodyFades in during moderate activity
Layer 3Percussion pulseFades in during combat

Intensity lerp

Music intensity lerps smoothly between peaceful (0.15) and combat (0.8).

Sound Effects

Laser fire
Short chirpy zap (bandpass-filtered square wave).
Explosion
Low boom with noise burst (when asteroids or enemies are hit).
Player hit
Sharp impact with distortion crack.
Engine thrust
Filtered noise loop, volume and frequency scale with ship speed.
Collector hum
Low sawtooth drone when the magnet is active.
Collection pling
Metallic ping on pickup.
