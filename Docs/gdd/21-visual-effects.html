Visual Effects and Color Palette

Status: done

This section ports the Visual Effects and Color Palette portions of "6. Art & Audio Direction" from the monolith GDD. It covers the screen shake system, the engine trail, the layered parallax background (twinkling stars, nebula swirls, black hole), the explosion effect, and the canonical color palette.

Visual Effects

Screen Shake

  • Triggered when the player is hit by enemy projectiles.
  • Trauma-based system: damage adds trauma (0 to 1), with quadratic falloff for intensity.
  • Camera offset oscillates at 25 Hz and decays at a rate of 4.0 per second.

Engine Trail

  • Glowing orange/red particles emitted from the ship rear.
  • Emission rate and brightness scale with ship speed.
  • Particles fade and shrink over 0.4 seconds.

Background Effects

  • Twinkling deep-space stars: 200 colored points with per-star brightness oscillation.
  • Nebula swirls: 5 large semi-transparent glowing clouds with additive blending.
  • Black hole: dark core with 4 spinning accretion rings (orange/red gradient).
  • All background layers parallax at different rates for depth.

Explosion

  • Explosion effect plays when asteroids or enemies are hit.
  • Low boom with noise burst on the audio side (when asteroids/enemies are hit).
