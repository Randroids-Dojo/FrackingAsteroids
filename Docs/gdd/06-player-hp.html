Maximum HP

The player ship has 100 HP. This is the starting and maximum value. HP cannot exceed 100 and cannot drop below 0.

Damage from enemy projectiles

HP is lost when the ship is hit by enemy projectiles. Each enemy projectile carries a damage value that is subtracted from current HP on impact, clamped so HP never falls below 0. Being hit also triggers screen shake (trauma scales with damage taken) and the player-hit sound effect.

  • Standard enemy projectile: deals the projectile's own damage value.
  • Ambush (prologue) projectile: deals 20 damage.

Drive-through repair at the gas station

HP is restored to full when passing through the gas station (drive-through repair). When the ship enters repair range of the station, HP is set back to the maximum of 100. The repair fires once per visit: a flag prevents repeated healing while the ship lingers in range, and the flag resets when the ship leaves the station range so a later pass repairs again.

PropertyValue
Repair amountFull (restores HP to 100)
Repair distance30 world units from the station
Repeat policyOnce per visit; resets on leaving station range

Persistence across saves

HP is persisted across saves. The hp field is part of the saved game state schema (an integer between 0 and 100) and is written and read back through the persistence layer. A default new game starts with hp: 100.
