Moon-Size Asteroids

Status: done

Moon-size asteroids are the largest asteroid class in the belt. They appear only during the cinematic prologue, where the maxed-out ship's lazer annihilates a dense field that includes 3 moon-size rocks alongside 30 ordinary asteroids. Outside the prologue they are never spawned.

In the prologue auto-mine phase, the lazer beam shreds asteroids including moon-sized rocks described as size 0, approximately 30 world units across.

Dimensions and collision

Size 0 asteroids (moon) are prologue-only, with voxel size 3.0 and collision radius 16.

PropertyValue
Size class0 (moon)
AvailabilityPrologue only
Voxel size3.0
Collision radius16

For comparison, the other size classes use a voxel size of 2.0 (size 1, large), 1.4 (size 2, medium), and 1.0 (size 3, small).

Hit points

Moon asteroid HP ranges from 25 (precious) to 80 (crystalline). HP depends on the asteroid type at size 0:

TypeMoon (size 0) HP
Precious25
Dense60
Crystalline80

Precious is the lowest at 25 HP and crystalline is the highest at 80 HP, giving the moon class a 25 to 80 HP range.

Voxel depth

Moon asteroids use 5 voxel layers for imposing depth. Where ordinary asteroids build their voxel cluster across the central z planes (z = 0 and z = plus or minus 1), moon-size asteroids add two extra layers at z = plus 2 and z = minus 2, producing the full 5-layer stack (z = -2, -1, 0, +1, +2). The extra layers give the moon class its imposing, rounded silhouette.
