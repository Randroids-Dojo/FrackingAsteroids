World, Visual Style, and Camera

Status: done

Visual Style

  • Voxel art: chunky, colorful 3D blocks.
  • Perspective camera: top-down view of the asteroid field.

Color palette

Deep space background
Dark navy/black with subtle star particles.
Asteroids
Earthy browns, grays, with gold/crystal highlights for precious types.
Ship
Metallic blue-gray with glowing engine trails.
Fragments
Bright, glowing particles matching asteroid type.
UI/HUD
Green and blue neon against dark overlays.

Camera

  • Fixed top-down view. Camera follows the ship with smooth lerp.

Build log

  • Voxel art style, perspective top-down camera, scene color palette, and smooth-lerp camera follow shipped pre-migration across M1 (Skeleton), M2 (Flight), and M9 (Polish). Perspective camera and lerp follow live in src/game/scene.ts (PerspectiveCamera(50, ...) at CAMERA_HEIGHT = 150, CAMERA_LERP = 0.08 applied each frame to camera.position.x/y). Voxel ship and engine glow in src/game/ship-model.ts (BoxGeometry voxels, SHIP_COLORS); voxel asteroids with per-type palettes in src/game/asteroid-model.ts (TYPE_PALETTES, ASTEROID_COLORS). Star field, nebula swirls, and black hole in src/game/background-effects.ts (200 twinkling stars, parallax layers).
